## Editorial approach

Use a seasonal occasion as the comedy hook, then teach a useful speaking task:
planning, personal experience, negotiation or reasoned discussion. Mix national
days with regional customs and seasonal celebrations; do not describe them all
as national holidays or imply that everybody celebrates them.

Every main title and visible opening hook must name the exam and level, such as
`DTZ B1:` or `ÖIF B2:`. Keep fictional comedy separate from the exam-style task.
Proposed exam labels below identify the intended book/audience, not an official
task or a completed implementation. Select a real source-book exercise and
verify the relevant exam format before authoring each episode. A fide topic
needs its own reviewed adaptation, not merely a relabelled DTZ episode.

Reuse the exam room, cast and optional dressing rather than building festival
crowds or a whole outdoor market. Add a few identifiable accessories and a
light joke, then return to substantive learner practice. Do not inherit the
Nationalfeiertag episode's special 100-120-second allowance automatically;
scope and measured pacing must fit the selected exercise.

## Halloween: recommended next episode

Halloween is the most immediate opportunity after Nationalfeiertag. Official
2026 listings show Halloween events in Berlin, Vienna and Zurich [H-DE, H-AT,
H-CH]. Position it as a shared seasonal topic, not as a national-day equivalent
or a claim that every household participates.

**Primary version:** `DTZ B1: Süßes oder Prüfungsangst?`

Retain the normal welcome, then add a short fictional exchange before the
practice:

> Samir: „Süßes oder Saures?“
>
> Prüfer: „Erst ein Vorschlag, dann die Süßigkeiten.“

The core is a complete jointly planned gathering, not just costume jokes.
Include the actual selected book exercise's constraints; the suggested venue,
guest list, budget, food, costumes and responsibilities are provisional until
that exercise is chosen. Let both candidates propose, respond, negotiate and
reach a concrete agreement.

For visuals, propose an LED-style pumpkin, small paper-bat wall decorations
and a removable witch hat. Keep faces, eyes and lips visible and the room bright
enough for readable exam practice. Extend optional `scene.dressing` only when
implementation is requested; these Halloween props are not implemented yet.
No new environment, horror sequence or full-face costume is needed.

**Alternative, not an additional approved production:**
`ÖIF B2: Halloween – Tradition oder Kommerz?`
Use a book-linked discussion with contrasting views on community, commercial
pressure, cultural exchange, costs and consideration for neighbours. Keep the
same light visual package, but do not pretend it is the same task as DTZ B1
joint planning.

Aim to publish on **October 29 or 30, 2026**, before Halloween on October 31.
This is a proposed window, not a promise that production will be finished.
If checked delivery cannot be completed in time, keep it as evergreen
seasonal practice or defer it; do not bypass speech/layout/native checks.
